Everything You Need to Know About ProwessBridge

ProCXel is the specialized recruitment and talent consultancy brand of ProwessBridge Tech Pvt Ltd. While ProwessBridge delivers enterprise IT consulting, SAP implementations, and software engineering, ProCXel manages recruitment across key industries, contractor staffing, and our proprietary ProCXel AI Screener ATS platform.
Our primary delivery and technology center is located in India, supported by dedicated client engagement leads serving organizations across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.
We offer flexible engagement models tailored to client needs, including Fixed-Price Project Delivery, Time & Materials (T&M), Dedicated Offshore Pods, Application Managed Services (AMS) with 24/7 SLAs, and Contract/Permanent Staffing through ProCXel.
